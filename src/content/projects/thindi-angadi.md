## Overview

Thindi Angadi is an online shop for an emerging B2C brand that sells snacks from across Karnataka. Visitors can browse by product or by region on an interactive map of the state, then shop with a cart, a wishlist and checkout. I designed and built the storefront and the backend behind it.

## Problem

The brand needed a storefront that felt as distinctive as its products, not a generic template, while still being easy to shop on a phone. It took a lot of planning to arrive at something unique that the client was happy with.

## My role

I built the website and most of the backend: the payment gateway, the backend wiring, a database set up to support automations, and cart and inventory management. On the front end I built the homepage, the catalogue, product, region, gifting and story pages, the cart and wishlist, and the Karnataka map. Gagan and Lavanya worked with me on the automations and parts of the backend. Codex helped with front-end implementation while I directed the design, features and revisions.

## Process

Most of the time went into planning and design, iterating with the client until the direction felt right. Once that was settled, the build itself took about a week. I refined the typography, homepage sections, product imagery and map interactions through rounds of feedback.

## Decisions

- **Mobile-first.** Most customers would shop on their phones, so every layout started compact.
- **The map as navigation.** Regional discovery is part of shopping, not a separate feature.
- **Editorial styling.** Expressive type and irregular image frames give the brand its own identity.

## Results

The storefront is complete, and a preview is live on GitHub Pages ahead of the brand's own launch.
