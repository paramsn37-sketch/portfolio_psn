## Overview

Kings Club is a tournament management app for a sports club client. It brings competition setup, member registration, brackets, scheduling and scoring into one responsive app for badminton, swimming and other sports. I built the whole thing myself.

## Problem

The club's earlier app had working pieces, but its UI was generic and each type of user had a disconnected experience. The goal was one professional app that makes club competitions easy to run for admins, officials and members alike.

## My role

I defined the product and built it end to end. The app is organised around three journeys: admins set up competitions and approve registrations, officials score matches, and members follow their events. I added sport-specific scoring, brackets, scheduling, responsive layouts and light and dark themes.

## Process

I started by auditing the earlier app, then rebuilt it from scratch as a pitch version. I built the first version in 2025 and updated it in 2026. It has been checked on desktop, tablet and mobile and builds for production.

## Decisions

- **Three role journeys.** Each user sees only the tools for their job.
- **Sport-specific scoring.** Badminton gets its own scorecard instead of one generic event page.
- **Light and dark themes.** Officials score at courtside in every kind of light.

## Results

The app is ready to pitch to the club. The next step is to gather their feedback and add more features from it.
