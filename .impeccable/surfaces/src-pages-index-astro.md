# Surface brief — The Personal Archive (desk + archive + case studies)

Scope: `/` (the desk), `/archive/` (chapters 00–06), `/archive/projects/<slug>/`. Mode: **Experience** (portfolio). Audience: recruiters, founders, peers arriving cold, often on phones. Action: find the work, understand the person, make contact. Content: placeholders until the owner supplies material. Constraints: brief-pinned palette, type roles, five mockups as the main template; other references only in small doses.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A person's archive you walk into from his desk at night. Refuses the category default of hero-plus-card-grid developer portfolio; the work sits inside physical paper objects, numbered chapters and a map, while the page underneath stays an ordinary scroll.

OWN-WORLD: Two lit worlds joined by one camera move — a walnut desk under a warm lamp (dark ground, brass, kraft, red wax) and warm paper (#F3EFE3) with linen rules, olive-deep controls, archive-blue ink, sticky-note yellow. Big Shoulders Display 900 uppercase headings, Hanken Grotesk body, Martian Mono metadata, Reenie Beanie notes, DotGothic16 for the name only. Tape, paper clips, polaroids, folders, a numbered chapter rail.

STORY: In one screen the visitor knows the name, what he does and where to go; choosing drops them into the paper; the chapters move from who he is → how he got here → what he built → how he thinks → who he is off the clock → how to reach him, with contact reachable from every screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desk fills the viewport; centred sheet (~60vw, 1000:645) carries a hand-drawn route map whose six waypoints are chapter links; centred on it: mono meta line, hand "Hi, I'm", PARAM S N at ~12.5% of sheet width, one-line tagline, primary "Open the archive", ghost "See the work", "Say hello" link. Six desk objects around the sheet are doors to chapters.

FORM: Pinned by the owner's brief and mockups (no tournament); ordered list position 1 of 1. seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: the camera zooms into the chosen object/waypoint and the paper becomes the archive (reverse zoom on return). Motion grammar: Emil Kowalski curves, press 0.97, lifts with lamplight glow, drawers on ease-drawer, page-flip for journal entries.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
