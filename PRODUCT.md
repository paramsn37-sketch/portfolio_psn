# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro + TypeScript, static output, deployed to GitHub Pages from `paramsn37-sketch/portfolio_psn` via GitHub Actions (confirmed by owner). Vanilla TypeScript islands; no UI framework, no animation library.

## Users

People meeting Param S N for the first time through a link: recruiters and hiring managers screening an AI/ML engineering student, founders and teams looking for a collaborator, and peers. They arrive cold, often on a phone, and decide within seconds whether to look further. (Inferred from the owner's brief; not separately confirmed.)

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio framed as "The Personal Archive": a place to discover who Param is, what he has built, how he thinks and what he does outside work, then contact him without friction. Success = a visitor understands who he is within seconds, finds the best projects immediately, can go deeper if curious, and reaches contact in one step from anywhere.

## Positioning

An explorable archive of one person rather than a résumé page: a desk to start from, chapters to walk through, case studies to open, a life to browse.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ordinary vertical scrolling is the primary navigation; no scroll hijacking, no loading screens.
- Every chapter and every project has a direct, shareable URL.
- Works with mouse, keyboard, touch and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Content lives in data files separate from components so it can be replaced without touching layout.

## Brand Commitments

Pinned by the owner's brief and five mockups (binding):
- Palette starting points: paper #F3EFE3, linen #D8D1C0, olive #737B58, archive blue #315D86, ink #252522.
- Type roles: condensed display for headings, clean sans body, monospace for metadata/chapter numbers/dates, handwritten notes, a pixel accent for the name.
- Materials: subtle paper grain, notebook grid, paper shadows, tape, paper clips, torn edges, folders, polaroids.
- Balance: ~70% clean functional, ~20% scrapbook/editorial, ~10% retro/playful.
- Chapter structure 00–06 with numbered chapter rail.
- Start page: an illustrated top-down desk with 2–3 central choices; the main choice zooms into the archive.
- Other references appear in small doses (retro OS window, analog console, stacked folder tabs, lanyard ID, sketchbook line art, fanned card stack).

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. Owner will supply text, portrait, photos and project material later. Every image slot and every factual line ships as a clearly marked placeholder. Do not invent achievements, employers, metrics, testimonials or hobbies.

## Product Principles

1. A familiar website underneath, a memorable journey on top.
2. Content is never trapped behind an animation.
3. Every visible control does something real.
4. Placeholders are honest and easy to replace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, full keyboard operation, reduced-motion fallbacks for every transition.
